Our Story

Established in 2022 and strategically located in Iperu Remo, Ogun State, Skaal Meats has rapidly become a trusted name in Nigeria’s meat processing industry. Our state-of-the-art facility reflects our commitment to quality, sustainability, and innovation.

We focus on safe, hygienic, and traceable meat production—ensuring that every product that leaves our plant is handled with precision and care. Our dedication to maintaining international food safety standards places us at the forefront of the Nigerian market.

World-Class Technology & Expertise

At Skaal Meats, we leverage advanced machinery and temperature-controlled environments to ensure the freshness and safety of our products. Our operations are supported by a highly skilled workforce—comprising technicians, quality assurance officers, and meat specialists—who undergo continuous training on hygiene, animal welfare, and food safety protocols.

These professionals work seamlessly across departments to maintain a clean workflow, prevent cross-contamination, and guarantee product integrity from start to finish.

Meat Processing Excellence

Our expertise spans across the processing of beef, pork, goat, and ram meat. Whether it’s bulk orders for large-scale distributors or smaller batches for niche retailers, we meet every client’s unique specification with consistency.

We handle every step—from humane slaughter and deboning to portioning, packaging, and cold chain logistics—with a focus on product traceability and freshness. Our vacuum-sealed packaging helps maintain shelf life and guarantees hygiene until the point of delivery.
